SWASTHYA CONNECT AI - how to get your link

1) github.com > New repository (Public) > "uploading an existing file"
   Drag in EVERYTHING in this folder (index.html, js, css, icons, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, .nojekyll ...). Commit.
2) Settings > Pages > Source: Deploy from a branch > main / (root) > Save.
3) After ~1 minute your link is shown on that page:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/

Demo logins: open the Sign in page and click a role button (password for all demo accounts: demo1234).
